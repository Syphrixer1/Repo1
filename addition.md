# Addition of Two Integers
Addition means combining two integer values together.

5+3 =8

The two integers are 5 and 3. 

The result of 5 and 3 is 8.


12 + 7 = 19

The two integers are 12 and 7.

The sum of 12 and 7 is 19

Multiplication means adding an integer value to itself, by a number of times indicated by a second integer.
